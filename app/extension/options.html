Connect your floating assistant

Keep Apprentice open in one tab. The orb controls that workspace; it does not capture or read the websites underneath it.

Website access lets the orb appear over ordinary HTTP/HTTPS pages. Browser settings, extension stores, PDF viewers and other protected pages may not support it.

Notes and questions stay inside extension-owned panels. Screen and microphone permission requests open in the Apprentice tab. Alt + arrow keys move the focused orb.
